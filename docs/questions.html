Frequently Asked Questions

Why not just use Serilog or NLog?

Traditional loggers are transport tools: they serialize and write whatever you give them. In a cascading outage, writing 10,000 exceptions to SQL Server or rolling files creates massive disk and database saturation. AvaranFlow sits ahead of your logging sinks to deduplicate, rate-limit, and aggregate repetitive storm exceptions in-memory first.

Can AvaranFlow crash my application?

No. AvaranFlow enforces strict failure containment. Any error inside a sink, database connection, or rule condition is swallowed, recorded in failure telemetry, and prevented from escaping to your host application threads.

What delivery guarantees does AvaranFlow provide?

AvaranFlow provides at-least-once admission into its in-memory buffer and best-effort delivery to sinks. It is an in-process resilience component, not a persistent distributed message broker.
